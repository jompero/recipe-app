Status: ready-for-human

## Steps (on the live site)

On an iPad in landscape and portrait, a desktop browser, and an iPhone:

- [ ] The top bar shows on the iPad (landscape) and desktop, navigates every section and shows each page's actions. iPad portrait and the iPhone keep the bottom button.
- [ ] Cookbook: several cards per row, sharp photos (after Regenerate, if 05 was built), wrapping chips, A–Z in columns.
- [ ] Recipe: two columns, ingredients stay in view while the method scrolls, scaling and ticks work, the screen stays awake.
- [ ] Plan: opens on Month on the iPad and desktop; add, move (menu and drag), remove; live sync with a second device.
- [ ] Drag on the iPhone: long-press drags, a tap opens the recipe, a swipe scrolls.
- [ ] Roll: the week grid on the iPad; lock, skip, roll again, save.
- [ ] Grocery list: comfortable column, amounts next to items, aisle sections, ticks sync.
- [ ] Data page: spaced sections, centred column on the iPad.
- [ ] The installed app on the iPad (if installed) behaves the same.

Anything that fails becomes a new numbered fix issue, `ready-for-agent`.
